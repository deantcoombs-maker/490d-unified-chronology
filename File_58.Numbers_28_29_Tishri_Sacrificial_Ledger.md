### 0.1 File-function

`File_58` records the Numbers 28–29 Tishri 1–22 sacrificial ledger as an applied demonstration of the Biblical List–Chronology Template.

The file argues that the bounded sacrificial list has two simultaneous functions:

1. a surface legal, ritual, and calendrical register;
2. a secondary printed-animal numeral ledger whose declared arithmetic may be projected into chronology.

The primary ledger produces:

```text
182; 189; 280
1274; 49; 490; 1323; 1960
```

The file then tests those constants against Jacob, Exile, Return, Exodus, Conquest, BJ / Jubilees, the MT / LXX / SP comparison field, selected Genesis animal lists, and a dependency-controlled Johannine endpoint proposal.

The file does not claim that every list automatically encodes chronology. It adopts the File_57 rule that a candidate list must be bounded, every load-bearing numeral must be accounted for, the ledger and sequence operators must be declared before chronology is tested, and alternative states must remain visible.

Argument-control rule:
Textual data and arithmetic facts carry the base layer. Structural inference may support the argument only when the list boundary, ledger state, category partition, sequence, scale, direction, and chronology operator are explicit. Historical-critical design hypotheses, typological readings, and providential synchronizations do not replace arithmetic or source control.

Interpretive-posture note:
The file does not raise the claim-status of a pattern merely because collateral evidence accumulates. Collateral evidence may make a reading more coherent within the local narrative and within ancient scribal or Second-Temple numerical habits, while the intended meaning remains unproved. The descriptive phrase `midrash-like` may be used only for associative, list-sensitive interpretation; it does not assert a formal rabbinic genre or date. Modern prose expectations are not treated as the sole standard for what an ancient list could communicate, but ancient intelligibility remains a historical-critical hypothesis rather than an arithmetic fact.
