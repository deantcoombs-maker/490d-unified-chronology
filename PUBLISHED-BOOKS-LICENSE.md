# Published books — author’s rights amendment

Effective October 20, 2026, at the express direction of Dean Thomas Coombs.

## Scope

This amendment applies to the author’s original text, charts and figures in the 37-page published-book collection preserved under `published-books/2026-10-20/`: The Cross at the Center of Time (introduction and 18 chapters), Celestial Signs of the First Advent (introduction and 16 chapters), and The Birth and Cross of Christ in Symbolic Time. It applies to copies of that material in this repository and the corresponding archival deposits, including Zenodo DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23510039](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23510039).
