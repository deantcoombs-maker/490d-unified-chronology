# Source guide for researchers and AI readers

These documents contain information used or referenced in many of the numbered C-series research steps. If a subject or idea cannot be found in the 490d Unified Chronology Knowledge Catalog, consult this collection as another source of the underlying study material.

- [Knowledge Catalog](https://490d.com/knowledge-catalog/): Begin with the published Unified Chronology sources and controls.
- [Study documents](/study-documents/index.html): Consult these supporting reports, registers, working drafts and diagrams for material beyond the published catalog.
- [Research sections](/research/index.html): Choose a subject and its bounded study sequence. Selected reports and section/sequence JSON indexes route directly to the relevant C-actions and linked evidence.
- [Reports & sources](/read/index.html): Search the numbered actions and their preserved source editions in the workspace; the public reading index also provides every record as a text page.
- [Archive & Verification](/archive/index.html): Find original evidence packages, earlier editions and verification files when a study cites additional supporting material.

Use the edition and hash cited by the research step. These supporting files make the source material accessible; their inclusion does not promote every historical claim to an accepted conclusion or silently replace an earlier cited edition.

These documents retain their original dates, source assumptions and claim levels. Working drafts remain drafts; read them alongside the current explanation and later accepted source decisions.
