Source guide for researchers and AI readers

These documents contain information used or referenced in many of the numbered C-series research steps. If a subject or idea cannot be found in the 490d Unified Chronology Knowledge Catalog, consult this collection as another source of the underlying study material.

  • Knowledge Catalog: Begin with the published Unified Chronology sources and controls.
  • Study documents: Consult these supporting reports, registers, working drafts and diagrams for material beyond the published catalog.
  • Research sections: Choose a subject and its bounded study sequence. Selected reports and section/sequence JSON indexes route directly to the relevant C-actions and linked evidence.
  • Reports & sources: Search the numbered actions and their preserved source editions in the workspace; the public reading index also provides every record as a text page.
  • Archive & Verification: Find original evidence packages, earlier editions and verification files when a study cites additional supporting material.

Use the edition and hash cited by the research step. These supporting files make the source material accessible; their inclusion does not promote every historical claim to an accepted conclusion or silently replace an earlier cited edition.
