# SP Noah–Shem Gear Knot — Canonical Register

## 1. Governing interpretation and scope

The 490d Unified Chronology examines how chronological traditions reconcile inherited figures, calendars, and representative roles. The SP mechanism registered here coordinates the conventional `600`-year span associated with Noah's cardinal age expression and the ordinal `599+` of his 600th year. Its purpose is to make those chronological conventions work together with the fixed anchors.

[Genesis 7:6 and 7:11](https://www.esv.org/Genesis+7/) supply the two number forms. Their organization into this SP field is the study's reconstruction. The conventional `480` used for [1 Kings 6:1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Kings+6%3A1&version=NIV), including File_05's `966+480=1446`, illustrates the same need to state how an ordinal expression is used in a chronological calculation.

Two distinct mechanisms are registered and must not be blended:

- **Flood Gear transport is cross-tradition.** Ordinary Gear displacement `−2 / 0 / +2` applies to the Flood start boundary in **MT, LXX, and SP**; each Gear's Flood year then extends one annual boundary from start to close.
- **The adjacent Noah/Shem rail is SP-only.** Within this localized SP field, the upper Noah/Shem station is primary and the lower companion is **primary minus one**. It does not transfer to MT or LXX, does not generate the Flood closing boundary, and does not turn the Flood into a two-rail object.

“Lower” means farther forward in time. The SP one-year adjacency does not create a fourth Gear or six independently attested source states. A numerical landing of a lower Noah/Shem calculation on a Flood-close coordinate does not erase the distinction between **person-station companion** and **event closing boundary**.

**Counting rule:** Attach the ordinal or completed-year convention to the interval and its named endpoints. A primary/companion column label does not prescribe one convention for every interval using that column.
