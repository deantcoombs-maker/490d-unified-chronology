# File_17 — Prophetic Time Span Anatomy

## 0. File-function

`File_17` defines the internal component structure of major prophetic time spans used across the 490d Unified Chronology repository.

The file treats prophetic spans as composite structures rather than monolithic integers. Some components are textually explicit, such as `1260 = 360 + 720 + 180` in the “time, times, and half a time” formula. Other components are inferred from repeated chronological, calendrical, and typological usage.

This file functions as a span-component reference. It does not replace the anchor-specific arguments in `File_00`, `File_04`, `File_14`, `File_15`, `File_16`, `File_18`, `File_19`, or the later Rounded Scaffold files. When a span is tied to an anchor such as `1876 BC`, `1846 BC`, `1446 BC`, `587/586 BC`, `1406 BC`, `701 BC`, `686 BC`, `967/966 BC`, `537/536 BC`, or `4176 BC`, the local state and node-class must be preserved.

### 0.3 Modal-state processing rules

1. A span-family entry is not a universal operator. It becomes executable only when the local section states the active state, tradition, node-class, and arithmetic operator.
2. Slash-pairs such as `931/930 BC`, `736/735 BC`, and `1662/1661 BC` are paired state labels, not averages and not ranges. Preserve order and evaluate component-wise if later arithmetic requires it.
3. En-dash ranges such as `1878–1871 BC`, `728–721 BC`, and `168–165 BC` are windows or historical ranges. Do not collapse them into a single anchor.
4. `1876 BC` is a shared year-label. In this file it may function as Entry, famine-window target, Sojourn start, or component endpoint depending on active state. Follow `File_16` and `File_04` where `1876 BC` is more than a component example.

6. `400`, `430`, `1260`, and `1290` may appear in the same Egypt-bookend field, but each value must retain its distinct node-class: Affliction, Sojourn, half-week, or extended half-week.
7. Prophetic-year, Enochian-year, Jubilee, and precessional readings are parallel component states. Do not use one calendar register to correct another unless a local operator explicitly does so.
8. Typological and theological notes may explain why a component matters, but arithmetic facts and stated textual data carry the computational burden.
9. Rounded Scaffold or Year-6 values are clarity-only unless a section explicitly opens the Rounded or Year-6 state. `4176 BC` in §13 is an MT `+275` / Year-6 comparison state, not a replacement Creation anchor.
10. Dependency-controlled cross-references such as `File_30` remain dependency-controlled. Do not infer missing derivations from a span-family resemblance.
11. The Priestly 336-day Ledger, Prophetic 360-day Year, and Enochian 364-day Year are distinct calendar registers. `336 + 28 = 364` may function as a bridge corollary, but it does not erase the 336-day baseline or the 364-day Enochian state.

13. File_14 biological-fractal values such as `270`, `275`, `276`, and `280` are dependency-controlled when used as gestation / purification states. In File_17, they may clarify span-component families but do not become universal component operators unless a local section opens File_14's argument.
