The Cross at the Center of Time

Book 1 · Eighteen chapters

This book begins with the different Passover dates in the Gospels and follows their calendars, numbers, and heavenly witnesses toward Christ’s Cross. I approach the earthly events with the heavenly world in view. The chapters build on one another; begin with Chapter 1, or choose a chapter below.

Read alongside Book 2

Book 2: Celestial Signs of the First Advent begins with Christ’s birth and follows the heavenly witness through Israel’s history and the Gospel’s movement among the nations. The two books share a concern to see the events of earth in the light of Christ’s heavenly authority.

Companion study for both books

The Birth and Cross of Christ in Symbolic Time connects the calendar and celestial themes of both books with the larger biblical chronology through day–year comparisons. It can be read alongside either book.
