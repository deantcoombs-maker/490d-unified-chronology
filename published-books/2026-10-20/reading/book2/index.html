Celestial Signs of the First Advent

Book 2 · Sixteen chapters

This book begins with the celestial signs surrounding Christ’s birth and follows their witness through the promises to Abraham, Israel’s deliverance and entry into the land, the Cross, and the Gospel’s movement among the nations. I read these earthly events with John’s heavenly perspective in view, asking how creation bears witness to the authority of Christ. The chapters build on one another; begin with Chapter 1, or choose a chapter below.
