### 0.4 Modal-state processing rules

1. Cumulative MT, SP cumulative, regular chronology, and phase-resolved Mirror coordinate-completion are distinct states. Shared numbers do not merge them.
2. Date-label identity requires agreement in state, node-class, tradition, suffix, and operator.
3. `4114 BC` is retained as the source’s compressed Eden-side anchor, but §3 expands it to `4115t/4114n BC` when the phase-resolved Mirror coordinate-completion is active.
4. The executable `5520` harmonic is no longer the old unsuffixed display `4114 + 1406 = 5520`. It is the component-wise civil cross-axis pair: `4115 + 1406 − 1 = 5520` and `4114 + 1407 − 1 = 5520`.
5. `AD 1406t/1407n` is the phase-resolved Mirror coordinate-completion of the Conquest-side pair `1407t/1406n BC`. The display inverts across the Mirror.
6. Generic phase-resolved Mirror rule: when a BC dual date is separated by a half-year as `Yt/(Y−1)n BC`, the AD Mirror display preserves the `.5` relation as `AD (Y−1)t/Y n`. It must not be written as `AD (Y−1)n/Yt`, because that changes the AD-side internal phase distance to about `1.5` years.
7. Formal proof rule: for proof-only phase notation, set `n = 0` and `t = 0.5`.
